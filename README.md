A MonoGame (C#) take on the classic Frogger. The frog crosses a highway with random traffic, then a river of logs, lily pads and turtles that submerge, to reach goal slots at the top. The game ends after 3 lives and shows a score screen.

Download and go to debug/bin/net9.0/frogger to play 

I used advanced OOP and SOLID principles ! Try it out and i hopr you enjoy as much i enjoyed in creating it and playing obviously !!!
